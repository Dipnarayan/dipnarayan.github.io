Dipnarayan Das

Doctoral Fellow @ IIT (ISM) Dhanbad
Dipnarayan Das is a C-DOT STAR Scholar of 2025 cohort in Computer Science and Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology (ISM) Dhanbad, focusing on security integration in fog and IoT networks. He served as an Assistant Professor (on study leave) at the Center for AI & ML, ITER, Siksha ‘O’ Anusandhan. His research interests span computer networks, cryptography, IoT, and security. He has authored multiple journal and conference publications, and serves as a reviewer for IEEE journals. He was nominated Academic Excellence award at the Indo Global Skills Summit & Expo 2017.
